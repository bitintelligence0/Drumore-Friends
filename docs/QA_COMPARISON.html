Brand book vs site

Each pair shows the brand-book page concept (left) beside the implemented site. The map views were refreshed 17 October 2026 at 1440px, 1024px, 768px and 390px. Images are in qa/.

First impression: cover → hero

DRU-6. Explore opens on the book’s cover, rebuilt as the site’s first screen: the full Gable Line mark with windows, “Drumore Friends” in Gelasio at display size, the descriptor, a one-line intro, the spring photograph (P016) on a mat with its Today · 2025 photograph tab, and the cover’s lifeline strip, whose Lamp line runs on toward the timeline. The book’s “working draft” line is dropped and the intro speaks to visitors rather than describing the brand. The header now carries the full mark (56px in a 76px Shutter bar; 40px in 60px on phones). The hero sizes to its content and the era bar starts 529px below it at 1440×900.

The Gable Line story

The book’s chapter 6, cut to one band under the hero: the gable-end photograph (P014) with the amber trace, and the three reasons. The logo sheet, sizes and rules stay in the book. Stacks on phones.

Main timeline

Same composition: era bar with planned widths, family lanes, portrait bubbles at the start of each life, evidence knots, the “raised by Lib and Thomas, from 1876” link and the marriage link appearing only for the selected person, and the docked panel. The site adds a Meeting and community lane, and fading ends where a life’s dates are not in the archive (the book drew those as arrows).

DRU-7, a timeline you can move through. The timeline is now a window on the era-weighted scale. “← Earlier” and “Later →” at the ends of the axis step one era and keep the selected person and year; drag (mouse), swipe (touch), sideways scroll, ⌘/Ctrl + scroll or pinch, the − / + buttons, ←/→, +/− and Home/End all move or zoom it between one era and all eras, and clicking a line’s chevron moves the window that way. The window stays in the URL as era, plus span=1884-1896 when it isn’t exactly one era; older links still work. Travel animates for at most 420ms with --ease and cuts to a 120ms crossfade under reduced motion. The SVG is now drawn 1:1, so sizes hold at 1024px: 60px rows from one constant (ROW in timeline.js), 18px portrait bubbles, 13.5px names, Gelasio lane headers, and at least 10px (16px here) between a chevron and its face. Hovering or focusing a line thickens it, darkens its name and shows a preview card; knots grow and show their date and title; a tap buzzes on Android. Every fade explains itself (“No death date in the archive yet”, with the last dated moment), known deaths get a small tick, Phebe keeps “1872 or 1874”, and an optional toggle draws unknown ends dotted to the edge of the window. The dock is a compact summary (portrait, name, relation, dates, one line, three moments, Open profile) and is never taller than the field. Checked at 1440, 1024 and 390px with no label over a knot, and with 30 people in a scratch copy of the data the timeline grew from 1272 to 2172px at the same 60px pitch.

Family tree

Same layout and portraits, now drawn from content.json.relationships. Selecting a person in any view selects them here. The site adds a “Centered” layout, which is the default on phones.

Map

The book’s map is a style study of an invented place. The site now uses OpenStreetMap tiles with proposed, unverified place geometry: area outlines, broad circles and a named road, with no exact site pins. Places without a reliable location remain in a visible list. The Map tab keeps the current selection across navigation and browser Back; map shapes can be selected by keyboard as well as pointer. The 1440px, 1024px, 768px and 390px captures show the visitor-facing layout.

One person’s story

Same structure: scene rail with evidence marks and the current scene highlighted, Walnut date line and Reported chip, Gelasio title and prose, quotation, original objects with provenance tabs, sources and the return pill. The site uses Codex’s five reviewed scenes and adds “Other dated moments” for events not yet written as scenes.

Story first, with the return pill pinned bottom-left.
